Tracking & Integrations

A funnel that can't report is a funnel that can't scale.

Ad platforms optimise on the data your funnel sends back. I wire that layer properly — so purchase events fire on the right step, attribution survives the redirect, and Shopify stays in sync.

01

Pixel & Conversion Events

Meta Pixel and conversion events set up across multi-step funnels — correct firing on checkout, upsells, and order bumps, not duplicated or missing purchases.

02

UTM & Attribution

UTM parameters carried cleanly through advertorial → landing → checkout, so you know which ad actually produced the order.

03

Shopify Sync

Orders, inventory, and fulfilment synced from CheckoutChamp or Funnelish into Shopify — including offer-ID and subscription mapping that most builds get wrong.

04

Subscription & MRR Logic

One-time and recurring products configured side by side — billing cycles, rebill setups, and continuity offers wired end to end, with payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal, NMI) connected and tested.

05

Post-Purchase Automations

Webhooks and triggers into email/CRM flows on purchase, upsell accept, and decline — so the back end reacts to what happens in the funnel.

06

Speed & Testing Infrastructure

Fast-loading mobile-first pages, plus A/B price and offer testing (Intelligems) on Shopify + CheckoutChamp stacks.
